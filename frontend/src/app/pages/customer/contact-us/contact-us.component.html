Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Carsey.in.

How can I buy a car on Carsey.in?

Visit our Buy Car section, explore available vehicles, and select a car to view its details.

How can I sell my car?

Visit the Sell Your Car section and submit your vehicle details. Our team can contact you regarding your request.

Can I book a vehicle inspection?

Yes. Visit the inspection booking section to submit your inspection request.

How can I get information about car finance?

You can submit a finance enquiry through the relevant vehicle details page. Our team can contact you regarding your enquiry.

How can I contact Carsey.in support?

You can email us at support@carsey.in for assistance.

Where can I find vehicle inspection reports?

Open the relevant vehicle details page to check the available inspection report options.
